## Everyday life and frustrations (2026)
- **KPLC**: token-buying failures, tokens "finish too fast" (KSh 500 under a week), planned cuts.
- **Water rationing**: repeated multi-day cuts across 100+ estates.
- **Traffic**: a 20-minute map trip takes an hour at rush hour; there's the Expressway.
- **Rent**: average about KSh 10k/month. Bedsitters: Githurai/Kahawa 6–9k, Roysambu/Kasarani 7–10k, South B/C/Ngong Rd 8–18k, Kilimani/Kileleshwa 20–35k, Karen/Lavington 25–50k. One-bedrooms run roughly 1.5–2× that.
- **Hustle culture**: "Maisha ni kujikaza". 71% of employed youth have side hustles. Thrift flipping on TikTok, boda (about 1.3M youth riders), Bolt/Uber, Glovo, egg & smokie stands (KSh 700–1,500/day profit), freelancing.
- **Betting**: crash games (Aviator) are a youth debt crisis. Show it as risky and costly, never glamorous.
- **Social obligations**: harambee, chama, funeral contributions read out to the shilling, ruracio (dowry), "black tax" to family.
- **Politics**: cash handouts on the 2027 campaign trail, the "wheelbarrow" jokes, *Niko Kadi / Tuko Kadi* (Gen Z voter-registration trend, 2026).
- **Gen Z**: grannycore thrift fashion, the MBOKA dance, TikTok as the main news source.

## Matatu / nganya
- Isuzu NQR 33-seaters, KSh 3.6–8M to build; graffiti, LEDs, screens, sound systems.
- Famous names: Bapesta, Opposite, Matrix, Money Fest. Rongai, Embakasi, Umoja and Pipeline routes.
- Nganya pages have 800k+ followers. Typical fare around KSh 100.
- Music: gengetone, amapiano (owns Saturdays), bongo.

## Food
Smokie pasua (Tom Mboya / Kimathi St), mutura (evening carts), chips mayai (late-night),
mandazi, roasted maize, githeri, nyama choma (Kenyatta Market strip near KNH), chapo madondo,
ugali sukuma, pilau, Eastleigh shawarma / camel / spiced tea.

## Sheng (used in game copy)
- Greetings: *rada / riang* (what's up), *manze* (man…), *oyaa* (hey you), *form?* (what's the plan?)
- People: *morio* (guy/friend), *msee*, *manzi / dem / denge* (girl), *buda* (dad), *mokoro / mathe* (mum), *mbogi* (crew), *donga* (boss), *mode* (teacher), *mtiaji* (hater)
- Money: *doh / chapaa / ganji / mullah*, *jongo / kioo* (100), *finje* (50), *rwabe* (200), *M-Sape* (M-Pesa)
- Places: *Kanairo* (Nairobi), *mtaa / zanabe* (hood), *keja / rotejo* (house), *ocha / mushatha* (village), *zabe* (hangout)
- Things: *mathree / ma3 / nganya*, *mrenga* (car), *ndula* (shoes), *luku* (outfit), *thiang' / tonje* (phone), *mbulu* (TV)
- Verbs: *omoka* (make it big), *shera* (dance), *banja* (talk), *gwaya* (be scared), *ngasha* (shower), *malo* (eat), *chai* (steal)
- Police: *karao / sanse / hessy*, *kanjo* (city council askaris)
- Vibes: *kiki* (clout), *imbo* (fake), *noma* (intense), *wueh* (exclamation), *niko kadi* (I'm set)

|---|---|---|
| Rongai (Route 125, Green Park terminus) | Baba Yaga, Detroit, Catalyst, Matrix (Damager), Batman, Mood, MoneyFest | Baba Yaga: dark and cream, John Wick theme, menacing front, custom lights. Detroit: Detroit Lions blue and white, 2025 Nganya Awards "Makeover of the Year" |

| Umoja (35) / Eastlands | Opposite, Bapesta | Opposite: red and white, often called Kenya's top nganya |
| Thika Rd / Githurai (45) | Bumblebee, Boombox | Bumblebee: yellow and black |
| Mood (George Ruto-linked) | Mood | Fully wrapped in graffiti (even the windows), LED bar and strobes, bucket racing seats in matching colours, a 65" front TV plus many 24" screens, subwoofers under the rear seats, DJ controller by the driver |

Interiors in general: bucket seats, screens, music-synced LEDs, subwoofers, Wi-Fi, sometimes game stations.
Artists use airbrush plus NC/Duco paint; themes are celebrities, films, musicians, religion and trending topics.
**2026 caveat:** a High Court ruling upheld a ban on matatu graffiti and tinted windows; MP Jalang'o is challenging it.
